## Summary

This agreement grants the client exclusive rights to design and frontend implementation work, with potential for unlimited revisions, and establishes a significant risk of IP assignment and potential liability.

## Clauses to negotiate

### Payment — HIGH

> Payment is due ninety (90) days after Client's written acceptance of the final deliverables.

**Why it matters:** This is a strict deadline for payment, leaving little room for the freelancer to manage cash flow.

**Ask instead:** Please confirm the payment schedule and provide a detailed breakdown of the deliverables.

### Intellectual Property — HIGH

> Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to all work product, including source code, designs, drafts, notes, and unused concepts, throughout the world, in perpetuity. Contractor waives any moral rights to the extent permitted by law.

**Why it matters:** This assignment of IP is a significant risk, potentially leading to the client having complete control over the freelancer's work and future projects.

**Ask instead:** Can you provide a detailed explanation of the IP ownership transfer and any associated licensing terms?

### Exclusivity — HIGH

> For twelve (12) months after the last delivery, Contractor shall not perform similar services for any company that competes with Client in any market, worldwide.

**Why it matters:** This exclusivity clause severely restricts the freelancer's ability to work with competitors, potentially impacting income and market share.

**Ask instead:** Could you clarify the scope of the exclusivity and the potential consequences of breaching this clause?

### Termination — MEDIUM

> Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason or no reason, with immediate effect and without payment for work in progress.

**Why it matters:** The ability to terminate with immediate effect creates a high risk of the client taking back the work and potentially causing financial loss.

**Ask instead:** What are the conditions under which the client can terminate the agreement?

### Late Fees — MEDIUM

> If Contractor delivers after any date named by Client, Client may reduce the fee by ten percent (10%) per day of delay, without a cap.

**Why it matters:** This is a substantial late fee, creating a significant financial burden for the freelancer.

**Ask instead:** What is the maximum amount of the fee reduction and what is the cap on the reduction?

### Scope — MEDIUM

> Contractor will perform design and frontend implementation work as described in emails from Client from time to time, including unlimited rounds of revisions until Client is satisfied.

**Why it matters:** The unlimited revisions and the expectation of continuous work create a high risk of the project being unfinished or not meeting the client's expectations.

**Ask instead:** Can you provide a detailed scope of work and a timeline for completion?

### Liability — LOW

> Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless Client against any and all claims, including Client's own negligence, with no cap on liability.

**Why it matters:** This clause provides a significant risk for the freelancer, potentially exposing them to substantial financial loss if the client is liable for damages.

**Ask instead:** What are the specific types of claims covered by this liability clause?
